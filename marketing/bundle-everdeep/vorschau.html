Two dark-fantasy idle RPGs, one bundle

Both games keep fighting while you're away, but they scratch very different itches. Grim Reach gives you a whole life in the Reach to tend: skills, gear, companions and a town of your own. Everdeep Idle hands you a class and tells you: the fighting is automatic, the build is yours.

Grim Reach

  • 22 skills from mining and smithing to weaving and trapping, all in one queue that can run your whole evening
  • Three combat styles chosen by your weapon, 110 creatures and 29 Champions to auto-battle
  • 23 companions, four playable races and New Haven, a town that works while you sleep
  • Real offline progress, no microtransactions, no energy, no paid skips

Everdeep Idle

  • Six classes, each with its own playstyle
  • Random gear, Uniques, Sets and endgame Artifacts, plus crafting that can perfect an item or destroy it
  • Triggers, loadouts and loot priorities that play your build for you
  • Hunts, delves, timed runs and boss trials, plus more than fifty pets to collect

Made for idle time

Set up a queue, close the game, come back to progress. Two very different takes on the genre in one bundle.
