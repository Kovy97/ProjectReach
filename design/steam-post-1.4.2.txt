Grim Reach 1.4.2 - Cinderfall Reborn

Hunters,

this update rebuilds the Cinderfall from the ground up, turns the Rewards Shop into something worth saving
for, gives the game a real home screen and fixes a long list of things you reported. Saves stay compatible
as always.

[IMAGE: Book of Scars, full tree]

CINDERFALL REBORN

- Because the Book of Scars is rebuilt, every scar you have spent comes back to you with this update. Open
  the Shard Altar and spend them again in the new tree. A marker on the Shard Altar button reminds you until
  you have seen it.
- Already went through a Cinderfall before 1.4.2? You also get back pay for the new scar sources: the gold
  you carried, dungeons and Slayer tasks of each earlier run, plus your companions, bestiary, achievement
  points and first raid clears, paid right now instead of at your next Cinderfall.
- New Haven no longer resets. Everything you built stays through every Cinderfall.
- The Book of Scars has grown to 24 gifts in five branches. New among them:
  - Long Night: +2 / 4 / 6 / 8 hours of offline time (up to 19 hours with the watchtower).
  - Practised Hand: gathering, crafting and thieving actions take up to 12% less time.
  - Mastery Embers: up to +100% mastery XP.
  - Emberblood: food heals up to 30% more.
  - Keen Eye: rare combat drops become up to 15% more likely.
  - Raven Post, Trapper, Seed Vault, Master Builder: more work orders, extra trap slots, up to three extra
    farm plots and faster building in New Haven.
  - Settled Accounts and Unbroken Trail: keep your auto-eat, auto-potion and bank slots, and a share of
    your Slayer points and Quest Tokens.
  - Echo of Companions: companions you don't have equipped pass on more of their bonus.
- Many existing gifts are stronger: more starting gold, bigger buried chest, more heirloom pieces, more XP
  from Apprentice Years and Second Nature, more damage and health.
- Scars now come from much more than levels. Each Cinderfall also counts the gold you carry, dungeons
  cleared, Slayer tasks and raid clears. Collections pay once, the next time you fall: every companion you
  own, every completed bestiary entry, every 100 achievement points and your first raid clear on each
  difficulty.
- The Shard Altar explains every source: what you have, what it is worth and how far it is to the next scar.
- Sign of Ash: every Cinderfall you have done gives +2% skill XP, up to +20%. No gift needed.
- The Chronicle of This Run that greets you after a Cinderfall now shows your deeds and levels as cards,
  with your level 99 skills up front.

[IMAGE: Shard Altar with scar sources]

REWARDS SHOP

- The shop is now one page: wares on the left, services on the right. Every chapter you finish adds new
  wares, and you can see what the next chapters will bring.
- New for Quest Tokens:
  - Reroll a Daily Quest: swap a card for another of the same difficulty and reward (2 / 3 / 4 tokens,
    three times a day).
  - Call an Order / Call a Large Order: the customer with the next work order of that tier turns up after
    your next action in a gathering or crafting skill. The shop shows how long the clock still runs.
  - Lesson: 30 minutes of XP in woodcutting, mining, fishing, foraging or thieving.
  - Master's Lesson: an hour of mastery XP for an action of your choice.
  - Companion's Trail: brings a missing companion closer, if you have the Slayer talent for it.
  - Fee Writ: 25% off your next raid fee, once a week (from chapter 20).
- Master's Lesson opens with chapter 4, Call a Large Order with chapter 9.
- The Contract Token for Slayer tasks is now on sale from the start instead of chapter 20.

DAILY QUESTS INTO THE QUEUE

- You asked for it: "Queue all dailies" puts every open daily quest into your queue, each card can be queued
  on its own too. If a craft needs materials you don't have yet, the gathering steps are queued in front of
  it.

[IMAGE: Daily quests with queue button]

SALVAGE, THE NEW WAY

- The shop has a new Salvage tab.
- Quick salvage: pick inventory, bank or both, weapons, armour or jewelry, then salvage all items of one
  rarity with one click. You see exactly what you get before you confirm.
- Mass salvage: your inventory and your bank side by side. Tick what you want gone and salvage it all at
  once.
- Locked items, equipped gear and items with runes are never part of a quick salvage. Items with a suffix are
  kept too, unless you switch that off.
- After every salvage you see what came out and where it went.

PINNED RECIPES

- Every recipe card has a pin. Pinned recipes show up in a small window you can move and fold away, with
  their ingredients, how many you can make, and buttons to start, queue or jump to the recipe.

SLAYER

- With "Get next task automatically", a new task is never left behind any more. If your fight can't switch
  to it right away because other plans are waiting, it joins your queue after them, ahead of any endless
  entries. Tasks finished in dungeons, raids or against Champions are queued the same way.
- If you fall against your current task monster, you try again after a short rest, as long as you have food
  in your auto-eat slot. After three falls in a row without a single kill the game stops trying.

QUALITY OF LIFE

- Auto-eat and auto-potion now take food and potions from your bank once your inventory runs out. No more
  falling at night with a full larder.
- The combat preparation shows how long your food will last against the chosen target.
- Monster cards show time per kill, kills per hour and XP per hour.
- Queue templates: save up to three queues and load them again with one click.
- The offline report tells you when your queue ran empty and how much time went unused.
- Coming back to a minimised game no longer freezes it for a moment: the time away is caught up in steps
  with a progress bar, and you get the same report as after loading.
- A small marker shows gear in your inventory and bank that is better than what you wear in that slot. You
  can switch it off in the options.
- Recipe pages can show only craftable or unlocked recipes and tell you what your next level unlocks. The
  level-up message lists the new recipes too.
- Quest objectives are clickable and take you to the monster or recipe.
- Optional desktop notifications when your hero falls, your queue is done or a Slayer task or large order is
  finished (off by default, only while the game is in the background).
- The Shard Altar shows up greyed out in the sidebar before you can use it, with what you need to unlock it.
- Kills during a Blood Moon that doubled your loot are now marked.

A HOME SCREEN

- New Main Menu page: your current story chapter, what you did last, your daily quests and the most
  important numbers at a glance.
- The button that leads back to the title screen is now called "Title Screen", so the two no longer share a
  name.

[IMAGE: Main Menu page]

QUESTS AND ACHIEVEMENTS

- The quest pages have new, cleaner cards and slimmer frames.
- The achievements page is rebuilt with categories on the left and a new card layout.
- Seven achievements that were hidden are now visible: Feathers Everywhere, Bull-Headed, Rat King, Old
  Grudge, Feeder of Armies, A Hundred Hours in the Reach and Ninefold.
- "Not Today" no longer asks you to die 100 times. Win a fight with 5% health or less instead.

FIXES

- A Cinderfall on the same day reset your daily streak and handed out a second set of daily quests.
- "Queue all dailies" queued too little when two quests needed the same action.
- The salvage confirmation could open behind another window.
- Tier IV runes did not show their fifth ingredient (Relic Dust), and with five ingredients the amounts
  could be cut off.
- The companion trail only counted kills in the companion's own combat style. It now counts every kill, the
  companion still drops in its own style.
- Bought items no longer pop up as a "Rare find".
- Rerolling a queued daily quest now tells you that its queue entries stay.
- Many smaller fixes for layout at 1366x768, number formats, and texts in all languages.

Thank you for every report. Keep them coming.
