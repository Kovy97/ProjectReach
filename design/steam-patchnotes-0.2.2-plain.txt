Update #1 — Quality of Reach (v0.2.2)

Thank you for all the feedback since launch. This update is almost entirely built from it: smoother automation, a fairer loot game, and a big round of interface polish.

✨ NEW

• Combat Queue — Fights now queue like everything else: pick 1×, 25×, 50×, ∞ or any custom number of kills, chain them with your crafting plans, and jump urgent fights to the front with Shift-click. Dying pauses the queue until you resume.

• Mass Sell — Tick items in the shop and sell them in one click, with a stack slider and a single gold total. No more one-by-one selling.

• Champion Victory Ceremony — Beating an elite now ends in a proper victory moment with your loot laid out. The elite tracker is a floating mini-window you can drag and collapse.

• Combat Log Filters & Resizing — Filter the log by category (your hits, enemy hits, healing, loot, XP and more) and freely resize the log and loadout panels.

• Character Screen Tabs — Equipment and Stats are now full-height tabs: a bigger equipment view and a clean, grouped stats ledger with proper tooltips.

• Readable Dungeon Cards — Every dungeon now shows its zone, a recommended combat level, and the actual creature levels inside. No more guessing.

• Blood Moon Rework — The Saturday world blessing is now a jackpot: every kill has a 15% chance to drop its ENTIRE loot twice, legendaries included.

• New Look — New game logo and application icon.

⚖️ CHANGES

• Dungeons are no longer level-locked. Only story progress gates them — the level on the card is a recommendation. The creatures inside are as deadly as ever; enter early at your own risk.

• Loot worth fighting for — Elite unique drops now match the level of their zones, high-level zones drop modern crafting materials, and dungeon clears can award on-level armor. Anything you already wear stays wearable.

• Healing rebalanced — Potions now scale meaningfully tier by tier, and all 46 foods have their own identity instead of healing nearly the same.

• Thieving is honest — Cards show your real success chance with a full breakdown, and loot odds are clearly marked "on success".

• Bank overhaul — Whole stacks move together when dragging, free quantity input, and a clear warning when your bags are full.

• Interface polish everywhere — New button set across the whole game, warmer wood-and-leather panels, bigger headers, unified typography, full keyboard navigation, and redesigned monster cards with grounded creatures and cleaner layouts.

• Performance — Significantly higher FPS in combat (especially on ultrawide screens) and a much cheaper combat log.

🔧 FIXES

• Monster cards no longer clip their name or hide drop icons behind the quantity buttons.
• Character stats no longer overlap at 1080p, and are no longer tiny on ultrawide screens.
• Elite portraits are consistent between the selection card and the battle screen.
• The floating champion window no longer sticks to your mouse.
• Card artwork no longer zooms in awkwardly — you see the full scene, with the creature standing at the bottom edge.
• Dozens of smaller consistency fixes across menus, modals, and tooltips.

See you in the Reach. More is already in the works — and as always: your save is safe with every update.
