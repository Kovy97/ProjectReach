Hunters,

Many of you took the time to report bugs and send me ideas, and your reviews told me honestly what works and what doesn't. You stayed patient and friendly even when something was broken. A lot of updates came straight out of that. When you missed the old interface after 1.3.0, 1.3.1 brought the Classic style back, and plenty of fixes began with one of your reports. Thank you for all of it.
