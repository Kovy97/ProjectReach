Grim Reach 1.3.0 - The Idol Hall Awakens

Hunters,

Drazhan withdrew, but his hall never stopped working. This update brings the Slayer's Book, a
talent tree for your hunt, and reworks every pet. It opens the Idol Hall, continues the Ash
Chronicle and raises the ceiling of the endgame across the board. It also brings everything that
was waiting in the wings: real bank slots, a Wiki of every item, a new combat HUD and a cleaner
interface. Saves stay compatible as always.

THE SLAYER'S BOOK

- From Slayer 85 you spend Slayer points in the Slayer's Book, a talent tree of permanent account
  upgrades.
- Tracking doubles your pet chance over five ranks. On the Trail guarantees a pet once you have gone
  twice the expected time without it.
- More rare drops from task monsters, more Slayer points per task, and bonus damage against
  families whose bestiary you have completed.
- The final node, Second Collar, opens a second pet slot for skill effects.

PETS, REWORKED

- All 23 pets now have a signature effect instead of "a few percent more". Cragmaw finds gems while
  mining, Gillrot pulls better fish, Gemwraith sometimes cuts a grade higher, Slagfiend adds
  suffixes when smithing.
- In combat every pet has a clear role: control, protection, damage, loot or support.
- Echo: every pet you own grants a quarter of its skill effect, even when it isn't equipped.
- Carapace is now as common as the other combat pets (1/80,000) and stronger.
- Seven pets were too strong and have been toned down: Ashgut's healing, Hoarspite's stun,
  Gloomrat's theft, Carrionwing's double loot (no longer on champion uniques and pets), and the
  ward, shield and lifesteal of Glyphmaw, Vellumoth and Charnel, which made mages nearly
  untouchable. No pet grants bonus XP any more.

THE IDOL HALL IS OPEN

- The Idol Hall is now an open hunting ground with seven new creatures, between the Highlands and
  the Sanctum in strength.
- Its two champions, the Ninth Warden and Hollowveil, can finally be hunted.

THE CHRONICLE CONTINUES

- Six new chapters (22 to 27) lead through the hall and into the Expunged Chapel, a new
  mini-dungeon. Something there is still at work on the walls.

A HIGHER ENDGAME

- Slayer Tower floors 6 to 8 at full endgame strength (Slayer 85, 90 and 95), with three new
  rewards in the Slayer shop. Tasks on these floors pay more points to match.
- New Paragon chest tiers from floors 25, 50 and 100.
- 18 new ranged and magic weapons at level 95 and above, so both styles get as many endgame options
  as melee. Five existing rare level-99 weapons were raised above their crafted counterparts.
- Woodcutting and Fishing reach 99 properly: three new trees, four new fish with matching dishes,
  and two new rods.

QUALITY OF LIFE

- Mastery no longer takes forever on cheap actions. From mastery level 50, every action earns at
  least 60 mastery XP per second of its base time, so the Normal Tree goes from 78 to 99 in about
  23 hours instead of 950. Nothing changes below level 50 or in the mastery pool.
- Ward Leech now shows in the combat log.
- Item cards tell you when an effect doesn't stack. Only the strongest source counts, except for
  the on-kill heal, ward and recovery bonuses, which add up.
- Enchanting warns you when the effect is already coming from another piece.
- The return report counts how many queue entries were skipped while you were away.

BANK, BAGS AND TOOLS

- Bank and inventory have real slots that remember where things are. Drag any stack onto any empty
  slot, also straight from your bags, and the list scrolls while you drag. New loot goes to the back.
- A fourth tool slot. Tools no longer belong to equipment kits and stay with your character.
- Bank upgrades are now sold in the shop, next to the inventory upgrade.
- Raven freight: send items between your own save slots.

INTERFACE

- A Wiki of every item, top right.
- A slimmer header with more room for the page, and pages that adapt to the space you actually
  have. No more words split in half, in any language.
- Reworked Slayer page, shop and token shop. Your combat style now sits in the sidebar, one click
  from changing it.
- A new combat HUD and a new return screen.
- Calmer, cleaner pages: frames no longer show a light ring at their edges, fewer frames sit inside
  other frames, and page headers follow one layout. The Slayer page now has tabs for the reward
  shop and the Slayer's Book.

FIXES

- The Crypt bestiary achievements can be earned. Saves that already have the kills unlock them on
  the first tick.
- "The Whole Bestiary" counts only creatures you can actually reach (now 126).
- Chapter 11 no longer completes itself before you enter the dungeon.
- Shock now respects immunities.
- The "{pct}" placeholder in effect descriptions is gone.
- The rare-find popup no longer hides real loot after you have crafted the same item.
- Enchanted gear in the bank gets its own slot.

As always: if something looks off, tell me here or through the bug report button in the options.
