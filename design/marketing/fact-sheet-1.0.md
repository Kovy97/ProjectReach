## The game in three sentences

Grim Reach is an idle RPG in a grim, ashen fantasy world: you set your character to work on one of
20 skills, and the game keeps working while you are away. What makes it different is that the
world does not stand still while you idle. 28 named Elite Champions surface in the zones on their
own timers and vanish again after fifteen minutes, and every real-world weekday carries a Blessing
that changes how the whole Reach behaves.

## What makes it stand out

- **The world hunts you back.** Champions are world spawns with a fifteen minute window, a live
  countdown and a bounty board. Miss one and it is gone. They only spawn while you are playing, so
  the design creates urgency without punishing you for logging off.
- **The real week is part of the game.** Seven weekday Blessings, from Blood Moon (richer beast
  loot) to Wild Hunt (faster attacks). Deliberately mild, no login streaks, no FOMO mechanics.
- **Loot with identity.** Rare drops are individual item instances that roll their own suffix, so
  two copies of the same sword are never the same. On top of that, 12 rune enchantments.
- **You build a settlement, and it pays you back.** New Haven has 15 buildings across three ranks,
  freely placed on a hand-painted map with a day and night cycle. Every building grants a permanent
  passive bonus, and the smelter and smokehouse keep processing while you are offline.
- **Finished, not staged.** No roadmap, no battle pass, no cosmetics store. The save compatibility
  promise held through every single update of Early Access and continues after 1.0.

## Good to know for coverage

- **Time to first hook:** a new character meets its first Elite Champion within roughly 20 minutes,
  and the very first equipment drop is guaranteed to roll a suffix, so the item system shows itself
  in the first few minutes instead of hour ten.
- **Watchable, not just numbers:** combat is automatic but staged, with three combat styles
  (melee, ranged, magic), three armor families (mitigation, evasion, ward), hand-drawn creature art
  and named dungeon waves. It streams better than most idle games.
- **Reference points:** players of Melvor Idle, IdleOn and NGU Idle are the closest audience.
