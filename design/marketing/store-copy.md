## 1. Short Description (Steam-Limit ~300 Zeichen — diese Fassung: ~285)

> A dark fantasy idle RPG where the world hunts back. Named elite champions roam the zones on their own schedule, daily omens reshape the land, and every rare drop rolls its own stats. Train your skills, push through crypts and dungeons, and build a hero the Reach will remember.

*(Alternative, aggressiver gegen den Klon-Vorwurf, ~240 Zeichen:)*
> Not just another idle game. In Grim Reach the world acts on its own: titled elite champions appear and vanish, daily omens shift the rules, NPCs react, and your loot rolls unique stats. Idle when you want. Hunt when it matters.

## 2. „About"-Block — Sektion „WHAT MAKES GRIM REACH DIFFERENT"

**THE WORLD HUNTS BACK** — Named elite champions surface in the zones on hidden timers, announced and gone again in 15 minutes. Daily omens change how the whole world behaves. The locals comment on all of it.

**LOOT WITH A SOUL** — Gear drops roll individual suffixes, enchanting adds rune affixes, and champions guard 28 named uniques with their own effects. Two identical swords are never identical.

**A CHRONICLE, NOT A SPREADSHEET** — The Warden writes your story as you play: your deaths, your absences, your victories. A dark quest chronicle gates the world, zone by zone.

**BATTLES YOU CAN WATCH** — Three combat styles, an armor trinity (plate mitigates, leather evades, cloth wards), status effects, pets that fight beside you, dungeons with waves and bosses.

**IDLE DONE RIGHT** — Action queue with stacking, full offline progress (deterministic, no cheating you), a mailbox so loot never vanishes, 7 languages, saves that always carry over. No wipes, ever.
