Grim Reach 1.4.1 - The Hunt Goes On

Hunters,

thank you for all the reports and reviews since 1.4.0. This update is built from them: Slayer points come
much faster and the Slayer tower finally fights back, work orders show up far more often, enchanting has a new
page, melee attacks got their own effects, and you can sell a mountain of gear in one go. Saves stay
compatible as always.

SLAYER POINTS, MUCH FASTER

- New switch on the Slayer page: "Get next task automatically". When a task is done, Brenna hands you
  the next one right away and your fight moves on to the new target, offline too. Before, a night offline
  counted at most one task. Anything else waiting in your queue still goes first.
- The Slayer's Book now costs half: 7,575 points for the whole tree instead of 15,150.
- The three Slayer tower uniques cost half as well: 750, 1,250 and 2,000 points.
- Already bought talents or uniques at the old price? The difference is refunded automatically the first
  time you load your save.
- World tasks now pay by effort instead of a flat 4 to 6 points. Long tasks on tough monsters are worth as
  much per minute as the tower.
- Every Champion kill gives 20 Slayer points.
- Every raid boss gives Slayer points: 10 on Normal, 25 on Heroic, 50 on Mythic.
- The first five floors of the Slayer tower are a real fight now. Their monsters had fallen far behind the
  world monsters of the same level and often died in one hit. They now have more health and hit harder.
- To make up for the longer fights, every monster on floors 1 to 5 gives more XP per kill, so your XP per
  hour there stays at least where it was. Tower tasks on floors 1 to 5 also give double Slayer points.
- Tower tasks no longer fill up with monsters far below your level. If the tower has too few targets at
  your level, Brenna mixes in world tasks instead.

SELL GEAR IN BULK

- New button "Sell equipment..." in the shop (Sell tab) and in the bank. Pick weapons, armour, jewelry or all,
  and a rarity limit. You see how many pieces and how much gold before you confirm.
- Locked items, equipped gear, kit pieces, pieces with runes, axes, master rings, gear bought with Slayer
  points and items an open work order still needs are never sold.
- "Select all" in the existing multi-sell mode now skips locked items too.

ENCHANTING

- The enchanting page is rebuilt. Pick an item and it stands on an altar with one socket per rune slot, its
  stats shown before and after the rune you are looking at.
- Runes are laid out as a grimoire with search and filters. Runes that fit come first, the others tell you
  why they don't.
- Everything from the old page is still there: removing runes, runes from the storage, groups.
- The page is the first to use our new painted frames. More pages will follow.

ITEM CARDS

- The buttons on the item card have slimmer frames, and the dark box behind them is gone.
- The item card is smaller and no longer grows that much with the text size.
- Click an item to pin its card, then drag the card by its title bar to wherever you like. Esc or the cross
  closes it.
- Axes are weapon and tool at once. Their menu now has "Equip as tool", so they no longer always end up in
  your weapon hand.

COMBAT

- Melee attacks finally look like the weapon you swing. All ten melee weapon types have their own swing and
  impact: quick cross cuts for daggers, thrusts with a shock ring for spears, wide sweeps for two-handers and
  ground-cracking blows for maces and mauls. Every type has two variants and its own critical hit.
- Weapons with fire, frost, lightning, poison, bleed or void effects show them in the swing, with an extra
  burst when the effect triggers. Your hero moves differently depending on the weapon, too.
- The hit chance is now labelled on the monster cards ("You hit 97%", "Hits you 48%") and shown in the fight
  header.
- The loot count on monster cards and in the fight opens the full loot list with names, chances and amounts,
  like in the bestiary.
- The "Go to monster" button in the Champion window now takes you to the Champion's card, not to the normal
  monster of the zone.

WORK ORDERS, MORE OFTEN

- Work orders now come in three sizes and show up much more often while you gather or craft.
- Small orders: 5 to 10 minutes of work, a little XP and gold, about every 40 minutes.
- Orders: 20 to 30 minutes of work, solid XP, sometimes a workshop crate, about every 4 hours.
- Large orders: 45 to 75 minutes of work, lots of XP, plenty of gold and a guaranteed workshop crate, about
  every 12 hours of skilling.
- Orders you already accepted keep their reward.

BALANCE

- Witchlight Tonic and Gloomspore Tincture are attack buffs. Their tiny health regeneration (12 and 36 health
  over the whole duration) did next to nothing and is gone, the text now says what they really do.

OPTIONS AND SOUND

- New option "Button style" with previews: wood, moss & wood, basalt & iron or leather & setting for the sidebar,
  top bar and category bars.
- The category bars you click to fold out recipes now look exactly like the sidebar and top bar buttons.
- The building list in New Haven has a new basalt and iron look instead of the wooden background.
- Text size is now a slider from 90 to 140%, with quick buttons for 100, 110 and 125%. Your old setting
  carries over.
- New option "Mute in background": music and sounds pause while the game is minimized or in the background.

FIXES

- Enchanted or suffixed gear you only own as variants now keeps its own inventory slot. It no longer jumps into
  a slot you just emptied, it can be dragged like any other item, and it stays where you put it after a restart.
- Dragging entries in the action queue is precise now. Moving an entry up used to drop it one place too high,
  and the list scrolled very fast at the edges while dragging.
- XP popups on skill cards no longer show numbers like +8.400000000000091.
- Hovering an item name on a skill card no longer makes a horizontal scrollbar appear.
- The top bar adjusts immediately when you change the text size.
- A tooltip on skill cards was only in German.
- Item cards no longer stay stuck on screen when the item under the cursor disappears.
- Long attribute names and the combat style in the sidebar no longer break in the middle of a word.
- The Champion button falls back to the normal monster if the Champion has already left.
- Raid runs in the queue now show the Slayer points of each boss.
- Enchanting no longer picks the wrong rune when the rune list changes while you choose.
- Several small symbols are now proper icons instead of emojis, for example luck and spell reserve on item cards.

More music is on the way. Until then: thank you for playing, and keep the reports coming.
