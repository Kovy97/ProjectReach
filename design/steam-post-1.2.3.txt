Grim Reach 1.2.3 - Font Selection & New Haven List View

Hunters,

a small patch, and nearly all of it comes from your reports. You can now pick the game's font, the
New Haven list view has been rebuilt from the ground up, the master rings finally carry stats worthy
of a level 99 reward, and the dungeon loot counter now tells the truth. Saves stay compatible as
always.

FONT SELECTION

- You can now choose the game's font. Options, under Display: Atmosphere (the house font, as
  before), Book (a warm serif), Antiqua (an elegant serif with wider letterforms) and Clear (a plain
  sans-serif, the most legible option). A preview line shows each one before you commit.
- Every option works in all seven languages. The bundled display fonts cover Latin only, which is
  why Russian and Chinese have always fallen back to system fonts; the selectable fonts were picked
  because they cover Latin and Cyrillic, and each carries a CJK fallback.
- The setting is stored per device, not in your save, so a smaller laptop can run a clearer font
  without changing anything on your main screen.

NEW HAVEN

- The list view has been rebuilt. It is now a proper building register: a scrollable list on the left
  with large artwork, rank and actions, and a full detail panel on the right showing the building,
  its effects, all four materials plus gold, and a single clear action. Building slots in the queue
  stay visible instead of appearing only when occupied.
- Refineries and the smokehouse now share one work window with mastered recipes, input, output, rate
  per cycle and how many cycles your supplies cover.
- On narrow resolutions the entries fold into two rows instead of running off the screen.
- The map view and its building popup are unchanged.

FIXES

- Dungeon loot counter: the combat screen showed "0/6" in a dungeon no matter how much you had
  actually looted, and drop icons stayed as black silhouettes. Dungeon enemies carry a variant id,
  and while the kill correctly recorded the drop under the base monster, the display looked it up
  under the variant id and always came back empty. It never showed outside dungeons because there
  both ids are the same. Your existing discoveries appear correctly from now on, no re-farming
  needed.
- Master rings: a player pointed out that the legendary Blademaster's Ring (Attack 99, 50,000 gold)
  granted less Strength than a Ruby Ring crafted at level 34. That was true for every master ring:
  they sat at 3 to 5 attribute points while the crafting ladder had been raised to 17. All 23 have
  been recalibrated. They deliberately stay behind the endgame rings, they are a mark of a 99, not
  a replacement for Godgem Ring or the Signet of the Ninth Warden: combat rings 12 points, skilling
  rings 9 alongside their master perk, and the Ring of Completion 16.
- Item card in Russian: the button row was capped at a fixed width, so the longer Russian labels ran
  past the card edge and "Sell" was cut off. Reported on the Bounty and Harvest Tonic cards. The row
  now uses the space the card actually has and wraps if a language needs even more.

Thanks for the reports. Almost every line above started as one.
