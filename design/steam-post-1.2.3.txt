Grim Reach 1.2.3 - The Return Screen, Rebuilt

Hunters,

the screen you see when you come back after being away has been rebuilt from the ground up. It used
to be a long column you scrolled through; now your loot is the centre of it, with everything else
arranged around it. Saves stay compatible as always.

WHILE YOU WERE AWAY

- The return screen is now a spread rather than a list. Your haul sits in the middle as the main
  stage, with progress and settlement work flanking it as narrower registers.
- Everything that happened while you were gone stays visible at once. Previously several events
  competed for the same spot; a companion find, a death and a halted job can now all be read
  together instead of one hiding the others.
- Empty groups disappear cleanly instead of leaving a hole. If you were only away twenty minutes
  and nothing but a little ore came in, the screen is short and says so.
- The layout steps down from three columns to two and then one as the window gets narrower, so it
  holds together on a laptop screen or in a small window.
- Rare finds are still called out separately from ordinary materials, and the offline cap, your
  New Haven cap bonus and the real time you were away are all still shown.

Nothing about what you earn offline has changed. This is purely how it is presented.

As always: if something looks off, tell me here or through the bug report button in the options.
