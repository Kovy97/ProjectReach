Grim Reach 1.4.0 - Feather and Steel

Hunters,

1.4.0 is a big one. The goblins have a capital, the bow finally gets the care it deserved, your workshop
comes alive, and the whole interface got easier to read. Saves stay compatible as always.

THE RAID: HALLS OF GOBKHA'RAZ

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-raid-boss.png]

- Defeat Drazhan and the goblin capital opens, built into the abandoned keep of the March. Five rooms, four
  waves and a boss each. At the end waits War-King Rakkzul the Heir.
- Three difficulties, unlocked in order: Normal, then Heroic after a Normal clear, then Mythic after Heroic.
- Every attempt costs a fee: 300,000 gold on Normal, 900,000 on Heroic, 2,500,000 on Mythic. Raid enemies
  drop no gold.
- If you die on Normal, the room restarts for free. On Heroic a restart costs 100,000 gold. On Mythic the run
  is over and you get a quarter of the fee back, and the same goes for giving up. Leaving the fight inside the
  raid counts as falling, and the game asks before you flee.
- Bosses change phase at 75, 50 and 25% health, and they tell you when.
- After every boss a chest opens: a 35% chance for a raid piece (50% from the final boss), otherwise
  materials. Then you rest: full health, time to sort gear and supplies, and on to the next room by button
  or automatically after three seconds.
- A Normal run takes about 15 to 35 minutes, depending on your gear.
- Each week brings a different modifier, and every boss has a guaranteed weekly chest on each difficulty.
- The queue and offline play work. Every run pays its own fee, and offline a Normal run restarts a room up to
  three times.
- Bring supplies. A full run eats a few hundred meals and some potions.

RAID LOOT

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-raid-loot.png]

- 31 raid pieces in three versions: Normal (godly), Heroic (a new teal rarity) and Mythic (a new pink rarity).
  Each version is a few percent stronger.
- Three sets with bonuses at 2, 4 and 5 pieces: Regalia of the Borrowed Fire (magic), Pitpack Stalker's Garb
  (ranged) and Bloodoath of the Crownguard (melee). Heroic and Mythic pieces count toward the same set.
- Raid jewelry has a main attribute that counts as Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence, depending on your weapon.
- The popup for godly loot now says "Godly find!". Heroic and Mythic finds get their own.

NEW SKILL: FLETCHING

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-fletching.png]

- Bows, bowstrings and cords move from Crafting into the new Fletching skill. Everyone starts Fletching at
  level 1. Crafting keeps all of its experience, and your mastery stays with each action.
- New: arrow shafts, twelve arrow tiers, barbed, venom and fire arrows from tier IX, and twelve quivers.
  Arrowheads are forged at the smithy.
- A Fletching master ring waits at 99.

THE RANGED REWORK

- Plain arrows are always there and never run out. Real arrows go into your quiver and add damage to every
  shot, and effect arrows add bleed, poison or burn. When the quiver is empty, the bow simply keeps shooting.
- The quiver is the off-hand for bows. It saves arrows and can refill itself from your storage.
- A quiver holds up to 1,000 arrows. Anything above that stays in storage.
- Crafted bows are a full tier stronger, and every dropped bow rises by the same step, so loot still beats
  crafting and raid bows stay on top. The defensive stance costs ranged 10% damage instead of attack speed.
  Every zone now drops leather armor and bows.
- Leather is stronger. Close to the evasion cap it softens the hits that still land, by up to 58% at the cap
  instead of 18%. A leather archer without raid gear now has a real chance on Normal.

MAGES IN CLOTH

- Ward now holds up. Whatever part of a hit your Ward takes is reduced by 30%. This only applies to the part
  the Ward actually absorbs.
- Every spell you cast recharges 4% of your maximum Ward, instead of a small fixed amount.
- Bleeding, burning and poison now hit your Ward first and only then your health.
- Mages in cloth can now last through long boss fights without switching to plate.

FROST AND BOSSES

- Dungeon and raid bosses are now slowed by frost only half as much (20% instead of 40%). Freezing is unchanged.
- One frost rune was enough to carry a mage without raid gear through Mythic. Frost stays strong, but no longer that strong.

STORAGE AND SALVAGE

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-storage.png]

- A new Storage page with 14 shelves. Every material has its own spot, with no limit, and it uses no bank
  space. New materials go straight there.
- Heads up: on your first load, materials move from the bank into storage. Your bank closes the gaps once,
  so its order will change. Bought bank expansions are not refunded.
- Looted gear can now be salvaged into tier materials and relic dust for the high runes. Only loot can be
  salvaged: crafted gear, shop items and master rings never can. You can lock pieces, and the game asks
  before salvaging anything legendary or better. Salvage works in your bags and right in the bank.
- A locked piece is safe from everything: salvage never touches it, and work orders never take it.

LIFE IN THE WORKSHOP

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-work-orders.png]
[IMAGE: 1.4.0-events.png]

- Small events now happen while you work: a Rich Vein, a Fish Shoal, a Masterpiece and more, plus Bountiful
  Harvest and Fresh Trail on your estates.
- Sometimes a customer brings a work order. Up to three run at once, with no time limit, and they pay gold,
  experience and now and then a workshop crate.
- An order only counts what you make or gather with its own skill after you accept it, offline included.
  Harvests, thieving and side finds don't count.

CLARITY

- Meals get their own slot in combat prep. Auto-eat no longer touches meals, and "Keep meal up" refreshes
  them in time.
- Every weapon shows whether it is one- or two-handed. Bows were never marked, and a few spears and maces
  had it wrong.
- Enchanted pieces now carry a rune mark, and rarity colors are the same everywhere.
- Farming and Hunting sit in a new Estates group with live status. While fields or traps are working, the
  header no longer says "Idle", and the offline report has its own block for harvests and catches.

A CLEARER INTERFACE

[IMAGE: 1.4.0-companions-readable.png]

- Text now grows with your window. The smallest text is much larger than before, and on big screens every page
  reads comfortably.
- Descriptions, effects, hints and quests use a new, easier to read typeface with real lowercase letters.
  Titles and buttons keep the familiar look.
- New option: Text size (Normal, Large, Very large), right under the font choice. It only scales text and is
  saved per device.
- Every page now uses its space. Action cards show what they produce in large, and companions, the chronicle,
  the enchanting altar, the shop, bank, storage and the offline report fill the room they have.
- The shop's item cards sit on a warm painted background and stand out from the shelves.

BETTER TEXTS

- More than 1,100 texts were rewritten and checked against what the game actually does. Over 130 of them
  were simply wrong, from talent and companion effects to achievement goals and quest targets. They now say
  exactly what happens.
- 974 items that had no description now have one.
- Terms are the same across the game and across languages. Slayer is always Slayer, and the bank and the
  new storage are never mixed up.
- Every change is in all seven languages. Russian and French now address you the same way everywhere.


FIXES

- If an older save was running an action without the required level, that action no longer keeps producing.
  It stops and tells you why.
- Cinderwretch Hide was the only item without a picture and now has its own.
- The item card buttons are reachable and fully labeled at 1366x768, and the header no longer wraps when an
  event is running.
- Many fixes in Russian and Chinese: grammar, plural forms and full-width punctuation.
- Inventory drag and drop: items behind a gap could show up one cell off, and enchanted items without a plain
  stack were no valid drop target. Every cell now sits at its real position, and you can drop anywhere.
  Thanks for the report!
- With Shift held, the comparison card no longer vanishes right away at 1366x768.
- The bug report window opened from the main menu no longer gets stuck inside the options panel.
- Skill levels no longer stick out of the sidebar buttons at large text sizes.

As always: if something looks off, tell me here or through the bug report button in the options.
