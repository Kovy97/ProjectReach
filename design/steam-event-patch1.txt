Patch #1 — Champions of the Reach (0.2.0)

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BODY (BBCode, ab hier alles einfuegen):

Thank you. To everyone who bought Grim Reach in these first weeks, and to everyone who took the time to write reviews, reports, and suggestions: this patch exists because of you. Almost everything below started as player feedback, and that is exactly how we want to keep building this game.

So here it is. Patch #1: Champions of the Reach — our biggest update yet.

[h2]TL;DR[/h2]
[list]
[*] Champions of the Reach: 28 named elite monsters now roam the world, each with a shot at its own personal unique item.
[*] Blessings of the Reach: a different world-wide bonus every weekday, shown right in the sidebar.
[*] 14 new weapon drops across all level bands — spears, two-handers, war axes, staves, scepters, and more.
[*] NPCs and the Warden now comment on what's happening in the world and in your journey.
[*] Action Queue: chain up activities (e.g. "100x copper, then 100x iron") and your character works through them, even offline.
[*] Mailbox: a full inventory or bank no longer blocks progress — overflow is saved for later.
[*] Download size cut from ~684 MB to ~130 MB, save loading after time away from 14.5s down to 1.5s.
[*] Dozens of bug fixes, including missing backgrounds on the default Steam install path.
[/list]

[h2]New: Champions of the Reach[/h2]
[list]
[*] Named elite "Champions" now roam the world on a per-zone schedule, deliberately staggered so activity drifts between zones. A corner alert ("A powerful enemy has been sighted in...") jumps you straight there, the zone gets a pulsing sword banner, and the champion's card sits at the top of the zone with a live countdown — it sticks around for 15 minutes, max.
[*] Champions carry titles ("Greytooth the Famished"), hit noticeably harder than regular monsters, but stay soloable in zone-appropriate gear. Rewards: a fat material bundle, a big boost to that zone's chase-item odds, and a 20% shot at the champion's personal unique — 28 new named items with their own effects, from Hungerfang (heals you on kill) to the Hollowveil Locket (converts damage into your own ward). Every drop is individually rolled. New characters meet their first champion within roughly 20 minutes, and champions only spawn while you're actively playing — going offline costs you nothing.
[*] The Hunt Package builds five systems on top of Champions: Brenna's Bounty Board (Slayer page) shows live champion locations with countdowns and a jump button, plus fresh tracks and quiet zones — every champion kill pays 1 bounty token. Traces & Omens flash a subtle sign on the zone map shortly before a spawn. Blessing synergy: Blood Moon raises champion unique odds from 20% to 25%, Wild Hunt shortens their respawn. The new Champion Codex (Bestiary) tracks sightings, kills, uniques, and fastest kill per champion — unseen ones show as "???". Your first champion kill and first unique find each deliver a letter from Brenna to your mailbox.
[*] Your very first equipment drop on a new save is now guaranteed to roll a suffix, so you see the item-instance system in your first few minutes instead of hour ten. Early champion uniques honor the same guarantee.
[/list]

[h2]New: Weapons worth hunting[/h2]
[list]
[*] 14 new weapon drops spread across the whole game — daggers, a spear, a stone maul, war axes, a two-handed greatsword, staves, a scepter, a wand, an orb focus, and three bows with very different personalities. Before this patch, magic had exactly one solid weapon drop in the entire game and melee had nothing between the early zones and the endgame. Now every level band has weapons worth farming for every combat style.
[*] Four of them are true chase weapons (roughly 1 in 700 to 1 in 900): next-tier stats at your current band's requirements, including a greatsword that makes enemies bleed and a giant-slaying great axe.
[/list]

[h2]New: Blessings & a world that talks back[/h2]
[list]
[*] Blessings of the Reach: the sidebar now shows a card for today's active world blessing. Every weekday brings its own mild bonus — Blood Moon (+15% beast loot, Sat), Ashfall (+15% gathering speed, Tue), Deadstill (+10% crafting speed, Wed), Rotmist (+10% rare drop chance, Sun), Foghold (+10% XP, Mon), Reach's Greed (+20% gold, Thu), Wild Hunt (+10% attack speed, Fri). It changes at local midnight; hover or click for flavor text, the exact effect, and a countdown.
[*] NPCs now comment on what's happening in the world — the Steward, the Merchant, Brenna, and the Warden react to today's blessing, an active champion, your death streak, or your Slayer streak.
[*] The Warden now speaks up at two of the most-seen moments: the offline report opens with a line about what really happened while you were away, and the death popup logs your death into the chronicle by enemy family, with the tone escalating the more often you've died.
[/list]

[h2]Quality of Life[/h2]
[list]
[*] The game now starts in fullscreen by default for the full effect of the new opening — and there's a new display option: Fullscreen, Borderless, or Windowed.
[*] New opening sequence: a studio card, rising embers, and the new title theme "The Knight Quiet Forest" — click or press any key to skip it.
[*] Equipment now has a third tool slot, so pickaxe, axe, and fishing rod/sickle can all stay equipped at once.
[*] Monster names in the zone overview are now color-coded by predicted difficulty (grey = no XP, green/yellow/orange/red), with a hover legend explaining the colors. The overview also shows 4 monster cards per row instead of 2.
[*] The top bar can now be resized by dragging, just like the sidebar, and tooltips app-wide use the wooden Citadel look and never hide under your cursor.
[*] Bag capacity now counts visible cells: multiple copies of the same rare item share one cell and cost one slot. If something truly doesn't fit, a red "No free space in your bags" alert fades in WoW-style, and special loot automatically overflows to your bank vault.
[*] Action Queue: chain activities with target amounts (e.g. "mine 100 copper, then 100 iron") and your character works through them automatically, even offline. Every gathering/crafting card now has a 1x/25x/50x/∞ grid that stacks straight into the queue. Right-click the running action to stop it, right-click a waiting entry to remove it.
[*] New Mailbox (4th menu entry, with a counter badge): if your inventory or bank fills up while gathering, crafting, or fighting, the overflow now goes to the mailbox instead of being lost.
[*] Crafting menus now start collapsed, with a search bar that finds recipes instantly. The inventory header got a full-width search, scaling category chips, and sort icons in one row.
[*] Item Wiki sources are now clickable — a recipe jumps to its skill page and highlights the card, a monster jumps to its zone and flashes the monster card.
[*] Enemy wards are readable now: a visible shield bar, "Absorbed" instead of a confusing "0", and a tooltip explaining that warded enemies take +20% damage from poison, bleed, and burn.
[*] Attribute cards on the character sheet explain what each stat does on hover, combat stats scale cleanly with your resolution, and guardian/tutorial boxes are noticeably bigger.
[*] Players updating from the previous version get a short "What's New" tour.
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]
[list]
[*] Download size cut from ~684 MB to ~130 MB — the entire art pipeline moved to modern WebP (and 7 bronze armor pieces that had shown as white boxes since launch are fixed).
[*] Save loading after time away: 14.5s down to 1.5s. Even a full 8-hour offline catch-up now loads in under 2 seconds instead of freezing the game.
[*] Combat animations now run on the GPU — no more micro-stutter from health bars and damage numbers. The Achievements page opens in 0.2s instead of 0.9s, gathering and brewing pages open instantly.
[*] The main menu now idles at effectively zero CPU cost, and the game window only appears once the first frame is ready instead of flashing up blank.
[/list]

[h2]Bug Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed missing background images on many installs — zone, monster-card, skill-card, and main menu backgrounds could vanish entirely when the game was installed to a path containing parentheses, including the default Steam path (C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam). This likely affected the majority of Steam players.
[*] The second ring slot can now be filled by drag-and-drop.
[*] Crafted bows are now grouped under their wood tier, and elemental weapon materials were renamed from "...rune" to "...shard" so they're no longer confused with enchanting runes.
[*] Fixed a wand + off-hand focus exploit: all wands are now consistently two-handed; existing saves are cleaned up automatically, with the freed-up focus returned to your inventory.
[*] Auto-Eat now switches to your next available food when the selected one runs out mid-fight, and Auto-Potion no longer chain-drinks healing potions at full HP.
[*] Combat pets now only drop for their matching combat style instead of all 7 rolling on every kill, fixing the first pet dropping roughly 7x too often.
[*] The "New" marker on items now only fires for genuinely new acquisitions — not when dropping items or restarting the game.
[*] Attribute descriptions corrected to match what stats actually do.
[/list]

If you run into anything weird after the update, tell us — Steam discussions, a review, wherever. Patch #1 was built from your reports. Patch #2 will be too.
