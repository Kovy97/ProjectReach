Grim Reach 1.1.0 - Patch #2: The Slayer's Ledger

Hunters, this is the biggest update since launch: the Slayer system got a full pass (tasks that actually challenge you, a shop worth saving for, a new cape and a contract token), dungeons can be queued like monsters, enchanting grows by fourteen Tier IV runes and rune stacking, cooking yields portions, farming plans its next crop, the reward shop opens chapter by chapter with real keepsakes, and dozens of interface fixes from your reports landed. Full list below.

COMBAT, SLAYER AND DUNGEONS

- Dropped gear now always beats crafted gear of its tier and carries an effect; drop rates are lower to compensate.
- Sixteen dishes now yield two to four portions per cook, and each combat style has dishes with a matching bonus.
- Every piece of gear shows its enchantment slots, filled and empty, and runes say what they fit.
- Searching in a gear stack no longer closes the box.
- Fight now or queue up straight from the slayer task, with the remaining count preset.
- A Zones button during combat opens the enemy list without ending the fight, so more battles can be queued.
- The removed from queue notice no longer returns on every load. *Dev-Notiz (Spieler-Report 1.0.2): `lastQueueSkip` wird vom Panel im State quittiert; die Persistenz prüft den Grund beim Laden gegen das echte Gate neu (`canFightMonster`). Neuer Test `queue-skip-load.test.ts`.*
- Starting a fight from the bestiary no longer drops the running order. *Dev-Notiz (Chef-Report): `startCombat` stellt endliche Aufträge selbst zurück, damit jeder Aufrufer sicher ist. Nur endliche Ziele (Sims starten Kämpfe direkt, ihre Queue würde sonst wachsen). `queue-forced-fight.test.ts`.*
- Gear you cannot equip yet shows a small red prohibition mark in the bottom left of its cell.
- Four items and the Tallyman rune promised gold from kills, which no longer exists; they now grant loot chance (items) or every fifth hit +50 % (rune).
- Slayer buttons now use the page's own button family, and the Slayer shop is sorted by level with the level shown on every card.
- The Slayer target in the header wraps instead of being cut off.
- Slayer tasks no longer hand out trivial targets: the tower window reaches at most 3 levels below you, a thin pool stretches upward first, and a tower that lags behind your combat level yields world tasks instead.
- Slayer armor now carries real armor or evasion values plus a slayer effect (bounty, giant slayer, heal on kill); two dead offers are gone and the draught unlocks at Slayer 20.
- The back-to-fight button now uses the proper button family.
- Dungeon cards now list boss drops and show the item type on every discovered entry.
- The dead defence skill boost on 51 items is replaced by constitution (jewelry, capes) or a higher class value (armor, shields).
- The update notice button, the dungeon Enter buttons and the fight buttons on monster cards now use the iron frame, the new standard button.
- The Hunter's Cape (Slayer 30, 150 points) fills the gap in the Slayer shop: +15 % damage against an unwounded target.
- The Contract Token rerolls your current Slayer task for free.
- Dungeons can be queued like monsters (1×, 25×, 50×, ∞ runs); a run counts when the final boss falls and the dungeon restarts at wave 1 until the target is met, online and offline.
- Taking gear off with a full inventory is blocked with a message instead of overflowing.
- The slayer shop shows rarity as the familiar coloured glow behind the item instead of a thin coloured border.
- Slayer gear now serves all three combat styles equally.

RUNES AND ENCHANTING

- Suffixes and fixed attributes now match the armour class.
- Fourteen new runes from Runecrafting 85 to 95, including the first jewellery runes.
- The enchanting table draws runes from storage as well.
- The item wiki and the variant pouch sit on the game's dark leather surface; the pouch is as wide as the wiki and lists every enchanted version with a large image, one line per rune, the roll, the requirements and its actions.
- Rune of Wasting now +35 %.
- The reward shop now sells one keepsake per chapter shelf, six idle riders and a consumable, all below rune power.

CRAFTING, SKILLS AND FARMING

- The token shop now has six chapter-gated shelves with 47 offers, plus ten late-zone daily tasks.
- Pick what a plot sows next; the plan waits with a warning if you lack the seed.
- Three new coal seams at 50, 75 and 90 yield 2, 3 and 4 coal per swing.
- Queue up what builds next in New Haven. Costs are paid when a build starts, not when it is planned.
- Queue names no longer wrap one letter per line.
- Smithing time now scales with bars used, so XP per bar is consistent. Smithing only.
- Each queued action shows the skill level you will have when it finishes.
- Queue names no longer hyphenate.
- The first item of each smithing tier now unlocks with its bar; items spread across the tier.
- Queue tooltips no longer vanish under the cursor.
- Easy dailies now stop appearing above a total level of 150 to 300, so veterans only get tasks of their tier.
- Loot chips on skill cards now show the quantity when an action yields more than one item.
- Five mid-tier kill dailies stop at total level 600.
- One button family across the whole game: iron frame for primary actions, the smaller Citadel frame for secondary ones, red text for dangerous ones; quantity buttons included.
- New Haven build cards and building info cards are larger with more readable text.

SHOPS AND REWARDS

- Achievement category emblems are now painted icons.
- The reward shop scrolls again, and shelves you have not unlocked stay hidden until their chapter.

INVENTORY, BANK, WINDOWS AND UI

- The iron frames no longer stretch half a corner bracket along the edge.
- The item wiki is large and readable, with Source and Used-for as card grids.
- In windowed mode the bank shows six full rows instead of three, and the sidebar fits or signals that it scrolls.
- The sidebar scroll hint now stays at the bottom edge and fades once you reach the end.
- The inventory grid shows exactly as many empty cells as you have free capacity.
- The options screen now looks the same in the main menu and in game: one iron-frame button family, toggles instead of checkboxes, language chips and the back button included.
- Recipe sources now show an "Open recipe" button, and the wiki remembers where you came from with a Back button.
- Bestiary cards are uniform, stats larger, loot sorted by chance and grouped, buttons in the game's button family.
- The item wiki is now a three panel workbench: sources, the item, and its uses, with a summary band and a recipe button.
- New Game, Load Game and character creation are full screens with fade transitions, a Back to Menu button and the game's steel frames.

OTHER FIXES

- The thieving loot box no longer scrolls next to four empty crates.
- The activity panel now holds both progress bars and uses the free space at smaller widths.
- Late game feasts now call for venison or wyvern haunch instead of beef.
- The main menu is now a war table: painted backdrop, command list on the left, the last save as a dossier with a big Continue on the right.

Saves stay compatible as always. Thank you for every report, review and screenshot; keep them coming.
