## English

### Short Description
A dark-fantasy idle RPG. Gather, craft, enchant and fight your way through a grim, forsaken world, then let auto-combat and offline progress grind on while you are away. Master 20+ skills, chase rare drops, and forge your build across melee, ranged and magic.

### About This Game
The Reach does not forgive. It rewards those who endure.

Grim Reach is a dark-fantasy idle RPG about carving a life out of a dying land. You arrive with nothing but calloused hands, and the world expects you to earn everything else. So you fell blackened trees and haul ore from cold mines; you smelt, cook, brew and stitch; you learn the patient trades that keep a body alive out here. Every craft feeds another, and one log, one ingot, one rune at a time, you become something the wilds have to reckon with.

Then you take up a weapon and walk into the dark. Combat is relentless and automatic, but never mindless: melee, ranged and magic each fight to their own rhythm, and the horrors of the Reach hit back hard. Survival is a matter of preparation. Plate to soak the blows, leather to slip them, cloth wards to swallow what is left, and enough food and drink in your pack to outlast the fight. Come undergeared, and the Reach will teach you why armor is not optional.

What keeps you here is the climb. Enchant your gear with tiered runes, gamble on random suffixes, and chase the rare drops that rewrite an entire build. Track every foe in a growing bestiary, take Slayer contracts for loot no ordinary kill will yield, and craft the equipment a stingy world refuses to hand you. There is always a deeper vein, a sharper blade, an enemy you could not have touched a week ago.

And it never sleeps. Your skills rise while you play and keep rising while you are away. Auto-battle, auto-eat and offline progress mean the Reach grows in the dark, ready for you when you return.

Play in seven languages: English, Deutsch, 简体中文, Русский, Español, Português (BR) and Français.

## English

**Why Early Access?**
Grim Reach is a deep idle RPG with many interconnected systems: skills, crafting, itemization and three combat styles. Player feedback is the best way to balance and polish all of it. Releasing in Early Access lets the community help shape progression pacing, difficulty and quality-of-life features while we keep expanding the game. We want players involved in what we build and fix next.

**Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?**
We expect Grim Reach to stay in Early Access for roughly 6 to 12 months. This estimate may change based on community feedback and how the game develops. We would rather take the time to get the balance and content right than rush the 1.0 release.

**How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?**
For the full version we plan to broaden and deepen the game: more zones, monsters, gear and crafting tiers, additional endgame systems, a guided tutorial and onboarding flow, and more visual and audio polish. We also plan to keep refining balance and quality-of-life based on Early Access feedback. Our goal is a larger, more polished experience than the current build, though specific features may still change during development.

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?**
Grim Reach is already a complete, fully playable game. The current version includes the full gather, craft, equip and fight loop, more than a dozen deep skills, hundreds of craftable items across 12 metal tiers, three combat styles (melee, ranged and magic) with auto-battle, rune crafting and weapon enchanting, collectible companions with unique battle actions, per-action mastery, offline progression, and multiple zones and dungeons. You can play from the very start through to a substantial endgame.

**Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?**
We plan to raise the price gradually as we add more content and features during Early Access. Buying during Early Access means getting the game at its lowest price. We expect the price to increase step by step as the game grows toward its full 1.0 release.

**How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?**
We want the community closely involved in shaping Grim Reach. We will gather feedback through the Steam Community discussions and our Discord server, ship regular updates driven by player suggestions, and prioritize the balance, quality-of-life and content changes players ask for most. Bug reports and feedback on progression and difficulty will directly guide our development roadmap.
