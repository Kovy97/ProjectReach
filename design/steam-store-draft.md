## 0) Positionierung (1 Satz)
Ein **grimmiges Dark-Fantasy-Idle-RPG**: meistere 21 Fertigkeiten, schmiede dich durch 12 Metall-Tiers,
verzaubere Waffen mit Runen und kämpfe in drei Stilen (Nahkampf/Fernkampf/Magie) — auch offline.

**Genre-Anker (Pitch):** „Melvor Idle trifft auf eine düstere Schmiede-Welt." (Vergleich nur intern als
Orientierung; im Store eigenständig auftreten.)

## 1) Short Description (≤300 Zeichen, Store-Header)
**EN:** Forge your legend in a grim dark-fantasy idle RPG. Master 21 skills, mine and smith through 12 metal
tiers, craft runes to enchant your gear, and auto-battle across three combat styles — melee, ranged, and
magic. Tame eerie companions with unique battle powers. Progress even while away.

**DE:** Schmiede deine Legende in einem düsteren Dark-Fantasy-Idle-RPG. Meistere 21 Fertigkeiten, grabe und
schmiede durch 12 Metall-Tiers, fertige Runen zum Verzaubern und kämpfe automatisch in drei Stilen —
Nahkampf, Fernkampf, Magie. Zähme unheimliche Gefährten. Fortschritt auch offline.

## 2) Long Description (Store-Body, EN)
**HEARTHFORGE IDLE** — From the first copper pickaxe to god-forged steel, every swing builds your power.

A slow-burn idle RPG with the depth of a full skilling game and the grim soul of dark fantasy. Set your
character to work, watch the numbers climb, come back stronger.

**▸ 21 deep skills.** Gather (woodcutting, mining, fishing, farming, foraging, thieving), process (smithing,
cooking, fletching, leatherworking, jewelcrafting, runecrafting, herblore, inscription), and fight.

**▸ A real smithing ladder.** Mine ore, smelt bars, forge gear across 12 metal tiers — copper, bronze, iron,
steel, blacksteel, mithril, adamantium, runite, bloodiron, titanium, aetherium, godiron. Hundreds of
craftable weapons and armour pieces.

**▸ Three combat styles, your choice.** Your weapon decides your style — crushing melee, precise ranged with
tiered bows and arrows, or mana-fuelled magic with built-in spells. Run out of mana and your staff fights on
until it recharges. Auto-battle keeps the grind flowing.

**▸ Runes & enchanting.** Hunt elemental essences from themed foes, craft runes, and enchant your weapons with
fire, frost, shock and more — stack effects for devastating endgame gear.

**▸ Eerie companions.** Bind grim creatures that don't just buff numbers — they trigger unique battle actions
mid-fight: a thieving rat that snatches loot from your enemy, a smith-imp that sets your blade ablaze, a
carrion beast that drains life.

**▸ Mastery & achievements.** Every action earns its own mastery for double yields and speed. Hundreds of
achievements to chase.

**▸ Idle-friendly.** Offline progress, auto-eat survival, generous AFK comfort — respects your time.
