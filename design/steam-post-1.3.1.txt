Grim Reach 1.3.1 - Choose Your Interface

Hunters,

thank you for all the feedback on the new interface from 1.3.0. Many of you preferred the old look,
and many of you missed how the sidebar and the top bar used to grow with your screen. This small
update addresses both. Saves stay compatible as always.

CHOOSE YOUR INTERFACE STYLE

- You can now switch between two interface styles at any time. Reforged is the new painted look
  from 1.3.0. Classic is the interface as it was before the update.
- Switch it in the main menu (top right, next to the languages) or in Options under Display,
  "Interface style". The change applies immediately, no restart needed, and your choice is remembered.
- Classic brings back the old sidebar with its steel-framed buttons, the old tab bar and header,
  the old textures and frames, the war table main menu and the old logo.
- Everything that came with 1.3.0 works in both styles: the Idol Hall, the Slayer's Book, the
  reworked pets, the Cinderfall, bank slots, the Wiki and the new combat HUD.

THE SIDEBAR AND TOP BAR SCALE AGAIN

- Drag the sidebar wider and its buttons, icons and names grow with it again, in both styles.
- The height handle of the top bar scales its tabs again, frames and spacing included.
- This is how many of you fit the game to your resolution, and 1.3.0 took it away. Sorry about
  that, it is back now.

SMALL CHANGES TO REFORGED

- Thinner frames around the sidebar and top bar buttons.
- A dark wooden backdrop with a soft vignette replaces the stone wall behind the pages.

Your save is not touched by any of this. The style you pick is a display setting only.

As always: if something looks off, tell me here or through the bug report button in the options.
