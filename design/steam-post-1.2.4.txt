Grim Reach 1.2.4 - Your Slots, Your Order

Hunters,

this one is about giving you back control over where your things sit, and about a few counters that
were quietly impossible to finish. Saves stay compatible as always.

BANK AND INVENTORY ARE REAL SLOT GRIDS

- A slot belongs to you now. Take a stack out and the empty space stays exactly where it was.
  Nothing shuffles up behind it.
- You can drag a stack onto slot 40 even when 39 and everything before it is empty. Only the
  placement moves; the contents never change.
- New loot lands after the last occupied slot instead of dropping into the first gap it finds.
  Gaps in front are used only when there is nothing free behind, so a full pack never loses a find.
- Sorting is a button, not a mode. Pick how you want it sorted, then press Sort. Between presses,
  everything stays where you put it. It is disabled while a filter is active, because sorting
  rearranges every slot and not just the ones you can see.
- Dragging in the bank now shows you what you are doing: the piece hangs at the cursor, the slot
  under it is ringed in gold, and the slot it came from dims until you let go.
- Your existing bank is straightened out once when you load, so you start the new system tidy
  rather than inheriting weeks of accumulated mess. After that, the gaps are yours.

FOUR TOOL SLOTS, AND TOOLS STAY PUT

- There is a fourth tool slot. The game has four gathering professions and only ever had three
  places, so one tool always had to ride along in your pack. The four now frame your head on the
  character sheet, two on each side. Speed is unchanged: a carried tool always counted the same as
  an equipped one.
- Tools no longer belong to an equipment kit. They hang on your character and stay put when you
  switch kits, so nobody has to own the same pickaxe three times over. Anything currently sitting
  in an inactive kit is pulled out when you load: into a free tool slot first, otherwise into your
  pack or bank. Nothing is lost, enchanted tools included.
- Equipment kits can be switched from the gear panel on the enemy select screen, so you no longer
  have to walk over to the character sheet to change loadout before a fight.

A NEW ENCYCLOPEDIA

- The large question mark at the right end of the top bar opens an encyclopedia of every item in
  the game, sorted into the same categories your bank and pack already use, with a search box.
- Nothing in it is locked. Look a piece up long before you own it. One click opens its full wiki
  entry with sources and recipes, and closing that puts you back exactly where you were, search
  text and all.
- It is part of the guided tour for new players.

COMBAT SCREEN

- The fight screen has a reworked heads-up display: a short weapon-appropriate stat line for melee,
  ranged and magic, a full stat sheet you can fold out, and slimmer bands for you and your enemy.
  Health, ward and attack now float freely above the figures instead of sitting in heavy frames.

FIXES

- The Crypt bestiary achievements were impossible to earn. The zone counts five skeleton variants
  that only exist inside the dungeon, and kills on those are credited to the base creature by
  design, so their own counters could never leave zero. Since the achievement takes the lowest
  count in the zone, it was stuck at 0 no matter how long you farmed. Both Crypt achievements now
  ignore those variants, and any save that already has the kills will unlock them on the first tick
  after updating. Only the Crypt was affected.
- "The Whole Bestiary" had the same problem from the other side: it asked for 110 creatures while
  the bestiary itself lists 105, because those same five variants can never register. It now asks
  for the number you can actually reach, and the statistics page counts the same set.
- Chapter 11 of the chronicle, "The Lord of the Depths", completed itself. It asked you to kill a
  creature that also roams the open Crypt, so farming skeletons for chapter 10 finished the boss
  chapter before you had ever entered the dungeon. It now asks you to clear the Depths of the Crypt.
  Chapters you have already claimed stay claimed.

As always: if something looks off, tell me here or through the bug report button in the options.
