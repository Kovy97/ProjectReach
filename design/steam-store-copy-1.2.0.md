## Short Description (≤ 300 Zeichen, aktuell 266)

A dark-fantasy idle RPG with 22 skills, three combat styles and a town that works while you sleep. Mine, smith, weave and trap, queue a whole evening of work, auto-battle 110 creatures and 29 Champions, tame 23 companions, and come back to progress. Offline, always.

## About This Game (Steam BBCode)

[h2]Gather, craft, fight. Queue it all.[/h2]
[img]panel_01_skills.png[/img]
Grim Reach is an idle RPG about building a character out of work. Twenty-two skills, fifteen of them trades that feed each other: ore becomes bars, bars become blades, hides and cloth become armour, herbs become tonics, bait from Fishing goes into Hunting traps. Every action has its own mastery, every skill runs to level 99, and one queue holds the whole plan. Line up 650 swings at a copper vein, 25 bronze swords and 50 goblins, walk away, and the queue tells you what level you will be when it is done.

[h2]Your weapon picks the fight.[/h2]
[img]panel_02_combat.png[/img]
Melee, ranged and magic each fight to their own rhythm, and the weapon in your hand decides which one you are playing. Plate soaks blows, leather slips them, cloth wards swallow what is left. Staves and wands carry an element, and every element does something of its own: burn makes the target take more damage, frost freezes on the third chill, shock delays the enemy's next swing, poison stacks, void drains, arcane resonates, aether refills your ward. Combat runs on its own with auto-attack and auto-eat, but the Reach hits back hard: come undergeared and you will learn what armour is for.

[h2]The Reach fights back.[/h2]
[img]panel_03_world.png[/img]
Ten zones from the pastures to the idol halls, a bestiary of 110 creatures, and 29 elite Champions that each drop a unique trophy. Seven dungeons run wave after wave and can be queued like any other job; the Paragon tower has no last floor. Slayer contracts, 119 quests, 36 gear sets, twelve metal tiers, fifty runes, random suffixes and rare drops that rewrite a build: there is always a deeper vein, a sharper blade, an enemy you could not touch a week ago.

[h2]Companions that do more than buff numbers.[/h2]
[img]panel_04_companions.png[/img]
Twenty-three companions, each earned from the skill it belongs to. Every one brings a passive for its trade and a real move in combat: a bear trap that makes the enemy bleed, a rat that steals loot from your target, an imp that sets your blade on fire, a moth that veils the enemy's eyes. They are rare, and the card tells you exactly what they do before you have them.

[h2]Human, Elf, Orc, Goblin.[/h2]
[img]panel_05_peoples.png[/img]
Four peoples with thirty painted archetypes each, in two versions. Your choice is a look, not a cage: every skill and every combat style stays open to everyone. Three equipment kits per character let you swap a full loadout, tools included, between fights.

[h2]Build a town that works while you don't.[/h2]
[img]panel_06_newhaven.png[/img]
New Haven is your camp at the edge of the Reach. Fifteen buildings in three tiers each turn raw goods into finished ones on their own clock: a smelter, a sawpit, a smokehouse, a seed garden, a trade post. Queue the next build, log off, come back to a fuller storehouse.

[h2]Made for idle time.[/h2]
