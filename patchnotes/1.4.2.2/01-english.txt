=== TITEL ===
Grim Reach 1.4.2.2 - Hotfix

=== TEXT (BBCode) ===
[p]Hunters,[/p][p]a hotfix for the things you reported after 1.4.2:[/p][list][*][p][b]Back from the taskbar:[/b] returning to a minimised game no longer opens the "While You Were Away" report. The time away is caught up quietly like before 1.4.2, just without the freeze. A small progress bar only shows up if it takes a moment.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Companion trail back pay:[/b] before 1.4.2, kills in another combat style did not count towards the companion trail. If you own [b]On the Trail[/b], every missing combat companion's trail is now filled from all your kills so far, up to half. The companion itself still only drops in its own combat style.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Damage over time:[/b] bleed, burn and poison ticks now show the damage they really deal, including bonuses from your gear.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Crates:[/b] Workshop Crates and black market crates show their description instead of a text key.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Daily quests:[/b] a claimed daily quest no longer keeps counting up.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Large screens:[/b] new option [b]Interface size[/b] under Options → Display (70 to 100%). It shrinks the whole interface so more fits on screen, which helps on 4K TVs and with high Windows scaling. The crop catalogue on the farming page no longer squeezes into narrow strips, and stat names on enemy cards no longer break in the middle of a word.[/p][/*][*][p][b]Recipe order:[/b] in Tailoring, Duskweave Robes now come before Runewoven Robes, and in Smithing, Voidsteel now sits at its level instead of at the very end of the list.[/p][/*][/list][p][b]Thank you for every report. Keep them coming.[/b][/p]
