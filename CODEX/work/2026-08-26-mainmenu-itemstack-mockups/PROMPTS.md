## 01 — Ashen Gate

### Main-Menü

Monumental symmetrical citadel-gate composition. Logo centered high inside a restrained black-iron arch. A single broad CONTINUE plaque centered below it with the current hero name and playtime in one quiet subline. Five smaller forged plaques form a balanced two-row command rail beneath. Keep the panorama visible through and around the architecture. Strong ceremonial hierarchy, low visual noise, corners and joins built from practical ironwork.

### Item-Stack

Armory-vault interpretation of the same gate architecture. Permanent compact item header across the top. Base version in a narrow left pedestal card; enchanted variants in a clean two-column armory grid occupying the remaining width. Every card has thumbnail, suffix, two-line stats, compact quality bar and small EQUIP / SELL controls. A thin filter rail sits directly under the header. Designed to compare at least four variants without scrolling.

## 02 — War Table

### Main-Menü

Oblique top-down war-table command composition while still reading as a conventional usable screen. Logo floats above the distant citadel scene. The lower-left contains a compact vertical command stack; the lower-right contains one selected save dossier with portrait, level, last played and a strong CONTINUE seal. Buttons resemble inset map-table plaques and wax-sealed orders, not loose floating rectangles.

### Item-Stack

Tactical equipment board. Left column is a pinned BASE VERSION dossier. Center is a dense two-column set of enchanted variant cards. Right is a narrow comparison inspector for the currently selected variant, with enlarged helmet, stat deltas and prominent EQUIP. SELL remains a small secondary action. Filters work as stamped tabs along the top. Excellent at comparison and inventory management.

## 03 — Black Chronicle

### Main-Menü

A dark opened chronicle ledger rests across the lower half of the panorama. Left page holds the selected save and CONTINUE; right page holds the other commands as clean chapter entries with small heraldic initials. The logo remains above the book and the scene remains visible. Book construction is heavy black leather, iron corner caps and restrained parchment inserts, not a bright medieval manuscript.

### Item-Stack

Matching black chronicle catalog. A horizontal item header is the book title. Left page contains the BASE VERSION and filter index. Right page contains four compact enchanted entries in a two-column manuscript grid; each entry uses a small helmet medallion, suffix heading, quality bar and tiny action seals. Clear page hierarchy, no tiny illegible prose, no decorative handwriting.

## 04 — Frontline Registry

### Main-Menü

Fast, pragmatic military registry. Logo and panorama occupy the left two thirds. A tall narrow command dossier occupies the right third with a large CONTINUE block, current character portrait and stacked compact actions below. Strong alignment, minimal ornament, functional iron dividers and one muted gold focus line. Built for quickest navigation.

### Item-Stack

Dense quartermaster registry for large stacks. Fixed top item summary, then a compact list-table with columns ITEM, ENCHANTMENT, SUFFIX, QUALITY, REQUIREMENT, ACTIONS. The base item is the first highlighted row, followed by multiple enchanted rows. Each row retains a meaningful helmet thumbnail and readable suffix, not a spreadsheet-only look. A slim selected-row preview sits at left or top. Highest information density of the five while remaining atmospheric.

## 05 — Ember Reliquary

### Main-Menü

Cinematic reliquary composition. Logo centered in the upper third over the bleak panorama. A large selected save/CONTINUE altar card sits low center with a subtle ember line; the remaining commands form a restrained horizontal navigation strip along the bottom. Spacious, dramatic and luxurious without ornate excess. The world artwork is the hero.

### Item-Stack

Curated armory showcase. Large selected helmet and full selected-variant details occupy the left forty percent.
