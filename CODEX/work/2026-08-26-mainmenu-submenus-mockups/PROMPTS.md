# Prompt-Set — 15 Main-Menü-Unterseiten

Erzeugungsweg: eingebautes ImageGen-Werkzeug im Standardmodus, ein Aufruf je Mockup.

## Gemeinsamer Serienkern

High-fidelity 16:9 desktop game UI concept mockup for the released dark-fantasy idle RPG GRIM
REACH. Use the supplied current screen as the content reference and the matching numbered main-menu
mockup as the design-family reference. Preserve the exact Grim Reach product identity: Ashen Iron,
blackened steel, soot-brown oak, dark leather, restrained bronze, parchment-gold typography and
small rarity/ember accents. Mature premium PC interface, practical hierarchy, readable labels,
believable materials. No mobile layout, neon, glassmorphism, bright fantasy saturation, generic
ornate gold overload or cropped primary controls. Full opaque screen.

## Familienkern

1. Ashen Gate: fortified niches, monumental arch, ceremonial hierarchy.
2. War Table: physical map table, wax seals, orders and dossiers, strong selected state.
3. Frontline Registry: narrow columns, disciplined alignment, maximum scan speed.
4. Black Chronicle: black leather folio, iron corners, chapter hierarchy, no handwriting clutter.
5. Ember Reliquary: large selected preview, compact carousel/gallery, restrained ember focus line.

## New Game

Show `NEW GAME`, `BACK TO MENU`, five numbered save slots, `EMPTY SAVE SLOT` and a `NEW GAME`
action on every empty slot. Demonstrate that the layout can later hold occupied slots without
becoming cramped. Keep the Grim Reach panorama and logo visible where the family permits.

## Load Game

Show `LOAD GAME`, `BACK TO MENU`, three occupied slots and two empty slots. Occupied examples:
`EDRIC`, `MARA`, `VAELEN`; each shows portrait, `TOTAL LEVEL`, playtime, `LAST PLAYED`, `LOAD` and
secondary dangerous `DELETE`. Empty slots are clearly disabled. Comparison at a glance is the goal.

## Character Creation

Show `CREATE A NEW HERO`, subtitle `EVERY LEGEND BEGINS WITH A NAME`, `BACK TO MENU`, a large
armored hero preview named `EDRIC`, `1 NAME`, `2 PORTRAIT`, `SHOW ALL`, `3 STARTING STYLE`, cards
`MELEE`, `RANGED`, `MAGIC`, and primary action `BEGIN ADVENTURE`. Preserve the same practical
portrait and starting-style content as the current game while redesigning layout and materials.
