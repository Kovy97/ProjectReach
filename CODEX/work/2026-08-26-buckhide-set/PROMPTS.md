## Serienkern

```
Game item icon, isolated on a fully transparent background (true alpha, no checkerboard, no
backdrop, no hands, no body, no mannequin), dark fantasy RPG inventory art in a muted "ashen iron"
palette: dark hardened deer leather in smoky brown and ash tones, dull antler horn, rough short fur
trim in grey-brown, painterly realism with crisp edges, soft light from the top left, no magical
glow, no particles, no sparkle, no text, no watermark, no frame. Single object, centered at the
bottom, filling about 85% of the frame height, edges clean enough for a 48 px icon. Early-game
ranger gear: simple, well-used, a step rougher than tanned leather, nothing ornate.
```

## Pflichtzusatz (in jeden Prompt kopieren)

```
Output requirement: a native transparent PNG with a real alpha channel generated as part of the
image. The canvas outside the subject must be genuinely transparent (alpha 0), with natural soft
edge pixels where fur requires them. Do not draw or simulate transparency. No checkerboard, no
solid-color background, no chroma key. No floor plane, pedestal, contact shadow, cast shadow,
border, text, or watermark. One isolated object, shown slightly from the front at a useful
three-quarter angle, no ground contact. No shimmer, glow, luminous rim, or magical aura.
```
