## Serienkern Idle

```
Full-body character art for a dark fantasy idle RPG, isolated on a fully transparent background (true alpha, no checkerboard, no backdrop, no floor), painterly realism in the muted "ashen iron" palette (soot, steel grey, tarnished bronze, worn leather, one restrained warm accent), soft warm tavern light from the top left, complete figure from head to boots with generous padding, facing screen-right, relaxed peaceful idle stance as if standing at a tavern table, no weapon raised, no combat pose, no magical glow, no text, no watermark. Same scale and framing as the player character roster (1086 x 1448 canvas, figure about 85 percent of the height).
```

## Serienkern Gesichtskarte

```
Bust portrait card for a dark fantasy idle RPG, head and shoulders, three-quarter view facing screen-right, isolated on a fully transparent background (true alpha, no checkerboard, no backdrop), painterly realism in the muted "ashen iron" palette, soft warm tavern light from the top left, calm expression matching the character's personality, sharp readable face, no text, no watermark, no frame. 1024 x 1024 canvas, head about 60 percent of the height, same face as the full-body art of the same character.
```

## Pflichtzusatz Alpha (in jeden Prompt)

```
Output requirement: a native transparent PNG with a real alpha channel generated as part of the image. The canvas outside the subject must be genuinely transparent (alpha 0), with natural soft edge pixels for hair and fur. Do not draw or simulate transparency. No checkerboard, no solid-color background, no chroma key. No floor plane, pedestal, contact shadow, cast shadow, border, text, or watermark.
```
