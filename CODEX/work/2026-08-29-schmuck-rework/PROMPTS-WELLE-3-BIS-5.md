# Welle 3 — Magie (10 Motive)

## Serienkern Magie

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: Grim Reach inventory jewelry icon. Create one single dark-fantasy jewelry object on a genuinely transparent background with native soft alpha. Match the supplied existing Grim Reach jewelry references in painterly photorealism, object scale, soft overhead lighting, subdued saturation, material detail and clean centered presentation. No ground, no cast shadow, no scene, no frame, no text, no watermark. **Equipment never glows: no glow, shimmer, aura, sparks, particles, magical light or emissive edges — this rule matters most on this series, because the theme invites it.** The arcane character must come entirely from carved material: incised runes cut into metal or stone, raw essence crystal inclusions, and woven cloth banding. The gemstone determines tier; the setting must unmistakably communicate a caster's jewelry through engraved runework, essence and cloth rather than forged plate or leather. At 60 × 60 pixels the silhouette must still read immediately as magic jewelry rather than a hunter's leather piece or a warrior's forged ring. Exactly one object.

### Ring-Regel Magie

Ring shown slightly obliquely from above, complete lower band visible, filling roughly 80–88% of image height. The band carries visible incised rune glyphs cut into its surface; runes are recessed carving with darkened edges, never lit lines. Stone seated in a setting of thin metal claws or a wrapped cloth cradle.

### Amulett-Regel Magie

Amulet shown frontally, pendant centered, cord or chain entering from and cropped by the upper edge, filling roughly 80–88% of image height. The hanging band is woven cloth or fine cord rather than heavy chain; rune plates, essence shards and cloth wrapping dominate the mounting.

# Hinweise zur Produktion

- **Dateiname = Item-ID**, `.webp`, 640 × 640, RGBA, echtes Alpha mit vollständig transparentem Außenrand.
- Bitte mit **q92** speichern, falls dein Werkzeug eine Qualitätsstufe kennt. Bei Welle 2 lagen die
  Dateien bei 140 bis 300 KB, der Bestand liegt bei rund 55 KB; ich musste alle 14 rekomprimieren.
- Kontaktbogen je Welle **neben** `diamond_ring.webp` und `onyx_amulet.webp`, dazu der 60-px-Bogen —
  der hat sich bei Welle 2 als der nützlichste Prüfbogen erwiesen.
- Der 60-px-Test ist die eigentliche Abnahme: Wenn zwei Stücke verschiedener Reihen bei dieser Größe
  verwechselbar sind, stimmt die Fassung nicht.
