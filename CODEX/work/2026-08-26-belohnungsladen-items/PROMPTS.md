## Serienkern

```

backdrop, no hands, no body), dark fantasy RPG inventory art in a muted "ashen iron" palette:
desaturated steel blue-grey, soot, tarnished bronze and silver, painterly realism with crisp
edges, soft light from the top left, no magical glow, no particles, no sparkle, no text, no
watermark, no frame. Single object, centered at the bottom, filling about 85% of the frame
```
